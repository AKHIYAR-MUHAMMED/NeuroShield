# 📜 ISO/IEC 27037:2012 Digital Evidence Handling Protocol Compliance

## Compliance Checklist & Protocol Standards

NeuroShield strictly adheres to international standards for the identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence:

### 1. Digital Evidence Identification (ISO/IEC 27037 §6)
- Unique SHA-256 cryptographic hashing at the moment of intake.
- Merkle Tree root generation for batch evidence submissions.

### 2. Digital Evidence Preservation (ISO/IEC 27037 §7)
- Immutable anchoring to Polygon decentralized blockchain ledger.
- DataStax Astra DB 1536-dimensional vector embedding store.

### 3. Chain of Custody & Auditability (ISO/IEC 27037 §8)
- Complete timestamped audit log detailing evidence examiner IDs, sensor hardware models, and analysis stages.
- Self-verifiable QR code certificates for courtroom presentation under Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE) 902(14).
